Property Description

Welcome to this exceptional Oceanview Luxury Villa, a masterpiece of contemporary design nestled in the prestigious Coastal Heights neighbourhood. Offering breathtaking panoramic ocean views from nearly every room, this residence combines sophisticated architecture with refined living spaces.

The open-plan living and dining area features floor-to-ceiling glass walls that frame the coastline, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor experience. A chef’s kitchen with premium appliances, custom cabinetry and a large island anchors the heart of the home. Five generously proportioned bedrooms include a luxurious primary suite with private terrace, spa-inspired ensuite and walk-in wardrobe.

Outdoor living is elevated by an infinity-edge pool, landscaped gardens, covered entertainment terrace and multiple alfresco dining zones – perfect for both intimate evenings and larger gatherings.

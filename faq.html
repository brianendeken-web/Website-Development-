Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about buying, selling, renting and more

How do I start the process of buying a property?

Begin by speaking with one of our agents about your requirements and budget. We can then arrange property viewings, provide market advice, and guide you through inspections, offers, and settlement.

What is involved in selling my property?

We start with a complimentary appraisal, then develop a tailored marketing plan. This typically includes professional photography, online listings, open homes, and skilled negotiation through to settlement.

How are rental properties managed?

Our property management service covers tenant screening, rent collection, routine inspections, maintenance coordination, and regular reporting to landlords.

Do you charge for a property appraisal?

No. We offer complimentary, no-obligation appraisals for properties we are invited to market.

How long does the average sale take?

Timeframes vary depending on market conditions, pricing strategy, and property presentation. Your agent will provide realistic expectations based on current data.

Can I arrange a private inspection?

Yes. Contact the listing agent or use the enquiry form on any property page to request a private inspection at a mutually convenient time.

What areas do you cover?

We primarily focus on metropolitan and surrounding premium suburbs. Contact us with your specific location and we will confirm coverage or refer you to a trusted partner if needed.

How do I submit an application for a rental property?

Once you have viewed a property, request an application form from the property manager. We will guide you through the required documentation and next steps.
